How the Review Process Works
1
Developer Submits via Form
A developer submits their product using the Product Submission Form. This triggers the entire QA process automatically.
2
Discord Announcement Posted
A Discord announcement is posted in the product announcements channel informing everyone that a thread will open for voting.
3
Product Added to Tracker
A new row is added to the Product Tracker for easy access. QA Manager+ can update the status when voting ends.
4
You Review & Vote
You will be pinged in the Product Discussions Channel. Share your feedback and vote on whether the product is ready. Use the checklist provided.
5
Accepted → Accepted Products List
Accepted products are added to the Accepted Products Tracker so all staff can assist Customer Support agents who need to reach the developer.
Your Responsibilities & General Criteria
