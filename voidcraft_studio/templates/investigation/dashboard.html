Standardised Case Submission Process
IA Activity Requirements
All IA members are expected to maintain an active presence and consistently handle investigation tickets. If you go inactive without filing an official LOA (Leave of Absence), you risk immediate termination from the Internal Affairs team. We are handling a high volume of cases — we only have room for active investigators.

IA Management will review your submission and accept or deny it via the bot commands. Do not resubmit while pending.
3

Once accepted, the case is automatically posted to the official case logs channel. It will also appear in your dashboard and the admin panel.

Management Posts to Public Blacklist
IA Management will handle posting to the public blacklist. Do NOT post to the public blacklist yourself.
