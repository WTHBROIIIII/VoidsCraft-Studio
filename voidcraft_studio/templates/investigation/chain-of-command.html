Chain of Command

Internal Affairs department hierarchy and organizational structure.

Tier 1 · Executive
Chief Executive Officer
Oversees all studio operations. Final authority across all departments and internal affairs decisions.
CEO
Tier 2 · Executive Support
Secretary
Manages executive-level communications, record keeping, and administrative coordination.
Secretary
Tier 3 · Management
Operations Manager
Oversees cross-department coordination. Bridges executive leadership and department-level management.
Ops Mgr
Tier 4 · Department Lead
Internal Affairs Management
Leads the IA department. Reviews and approves case drafts, does final review, posts to the public blacklist. Assigns case scoring and manages team performance.
IA Mgmt
Tier 5 · Senior Agent
Senior Internal Affairs Agent
Experienced investigators handling complex cases. Mentors junior agents and assists management with case reviews.
Senior IA
Tier 6 · Agent
Internal Affairs Agent
Core investigators. Handle standard cases independently. Submit via proper review process.
IA Agent
Tier 7 · Junior
Junior Internal Affairs Agent
Entry-level agents. Building skills under supervision. Must maintain active case history to advance.
Junior IA
