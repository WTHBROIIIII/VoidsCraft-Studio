How to Solve Tickets
1
Opening Line — No Problem Stated Yet
If the ticket opener hasn't said anything yet, use:
"Hey! I'm [your name] from the customer support department, how may I assist you today?"
2
Opening Line — Problem Already Stated
If they've described their issue, use:
"Hey! I'm [your name] from the customer support department, I will be assisting you with this ticket."
3
Wrong Ticket Type?
If a user opened the wrong ticket type (e.g. general ticket for a purchase), tell them to open the correct one. Use your judgment — if it's a question, solve it yourself regardless of ticket category.
4
Ticket Transfer
If you cannot solve the issue, ask a colleague in the staff server if they can take over. This is teamwork, not a competition.
5
How to Log
Download the transcript from the transcripts channel in the main server (after saving transcript), then log it in the ticket logs channel.
